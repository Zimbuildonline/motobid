Who we are, and who we are not

We are a buying agent

We scout cars across official South African auction houses and post the ones worth buying here, for our clients to view.

We bid for you

When you choose a car, we register and bid for you on the official auction's own platform, as your declared agent with your signed authority.

We are not the auction or the seller

We don't own these cars and we're not affiliated with any auction house. Each car is sold by its auction, under that auction's rules and fees.

Prices are not fixed

The starting bid shown is where bidding opens. The final price is set by bidding on auction day, and we only bid up to the maximum you set.

Accident-damaged cars: your choice

Most auction cars have some accident damage. That is why they sell below market price. Every car we list shows its damage, and you choose how to handle it.

Option A

We repair it in SA

We work with reputable panel beaters in South Africa. We get you a written quote before any work starts, you approve it, and we oversee the repair. The car arrives in Zimbabwe fixed. Repair costs are paid at cost, with the panel beater's invoice.

Option B

You repair it at home

We bring the car to Beitbridge as bought and you use your own panel beater in Zimbabwe. Often cheaper on labour, and you control the work.

Option C

Import it as it is

For parts, resale or a later repair. You pay only for the car, transport, duty and our fee. Damaged cars may need a trailer or truck to move.

Damage notes come from the auction's report and our own viewing where the yard allows. Hidden damage is possible on any auction car, so budget a margin for surprises.

Import rules to know

10-year limit

Private used vehicles older than 10 years from manufacture can't be imported into Zimbabwe, and an older one must be re-exported at the owner's cost. Vehicles over 3 tonnes are not affected by this limit. We only bid on vehicles that will clear.

Bidding for you, in writing

South African auction rules say anyone bidding for another person must declare it and hold a signed letter of authority. We send you one before every auction.

Duty on CIF value

ZIMRA charges duty on the car's cost plus transport and insurance to the border, then surtax on older cars and 15% VAT on top.

Sold as is

Auction cars are sold voetstoots. We inspect where the yard allows and tell you what we see, but there is no warranty from the auction.

What if I win but don't pay?

Once the auction is won, the full balance must reach us within 48 hours of bidding closing. The auction holds us to that deadline, so if the balance isn't paid in 48 hours your deposit is forfeited and the car goes back to the auction. Only set a maximum you can pay within two days.

What if I'm outbid?

We never go above your maximum. Your deposit is refunded within 3 working days, or kept on file for your next car if you prefer.

Can you find a car that isn't listed?

Yes. Choose "Find me a car" in the form and tell us the make, model, year range and budget. We watch the auctions and send you matches.

Why can't I just bid myself?

SA auctions need bidders to register with South African documents and pay from an SA account. We hold that registration and bid as your declared agent.

How long until the car reaches me?

Usually one to two weeks to Beitbridge after bidding closes, depending on payment clearing and collection from the yard. You then clear the car at the border. If you want it delivered further, we quote that per car and location.

What documents do I need?

A copy of your ID or passport, the signed letter of authority, and proof of deposit. We handle the auction invoice and SA export papers. ZIMRA clearing at the border is done by you or your clearing agent, at your cost.
